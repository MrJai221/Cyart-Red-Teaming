### NIST CSF Mapping: WannaCry Ransomware
| Function             | Action 

| **Identify**         | Catalog SMB v1 usage across the network. 
| **Protect**          | Disable SMB v1 and apply MS17-010 patches. 
| **Detect**           | Monitor for massive file rename operations (encryption). 
| **Respond**          | Isolate infected hosts from the VLAN. 
| **Recover**          | Restore data from offline immutable backups.
